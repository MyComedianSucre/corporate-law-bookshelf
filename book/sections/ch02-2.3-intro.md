## 2.3 Compliance and Enforcement  
〔ch02-p0042；[EPUB 原文](../epub_source/The_Anatomy_-_Corporate_Law_split_000.html)〕

<a id="ch02-p0043"></a>
Legal strategies are relevant only to the extent that they induce compliance. In this regard, each strategy depends on the existence of other legal institutions—such as courts, regulators, and procedural rules—to secure enforcement of the legal norms. In this section, we consider the relationship between enforcement and compliance. We then discuss three modalities by which enforcement may be effected.
