## 6.2 Legal Strategies for Related-Party Transactions  
〔ch06-p0014；[EPUB 原文](../epub_source/The_Anatomy_-_Corporate_Law_split_002.html)〕

<a id="ch06-p0015"></a>
Corporate law adopts a wide range of legal strategies to constrain related-party transactions which cover four of the five sets of legal strategies set forth in [Chapter 2](../epub_source/The_Anatomy_-_Corporate_Law_split_000.html#filepos138616): affiliation terms (mandatory disclosure and dissolution rights), agent incentives (trusteeship in particular), decision rights (shareholder approval), as well as agent constraints (rules and standards).
